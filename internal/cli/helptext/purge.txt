Permanently remove a ticket: its fields, comments, labels, history, and every
edge to or from it. `lit delete` hides a ticket and `lit restore` brings it
back; a purge cannot be undone.

What remains is a purge record that names the ticket by its id and creation
time and holds nothing of its content. Sync carries the record to every store
lit syncs with, and each store that receives it drops the ticket, even one
that edited the ticket in the meantime; a different ticket that another store
created under the same id is not dropped. Restoring an older backup does not
bring it back, and the id is never issued again. Other tickets are not edited:
a description, comment, follow-up or duplicate that mentions the id keeps the
mention.

An epic is refused while purging it would leave a ticket under it outside any
epic; the refusal names those tickets. Move them to another epic with
`lit parent set`, or purge them first. A child epic does not block the purge;
it becomes top-level.

Copies made before the purge are not reached: past Dolt commits, the store's
history on the git remote (refs/dolt/data), `lit snapshots`, and JSON backups
still hold the ticket until they are pruned.

At a terminal the purge asks for confirmation; elsewhere, pass --yes.
