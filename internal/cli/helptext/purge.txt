Permanently remove a ticket: its fields, comments, labels, history, and every
edge to or from it. `lit delete` hides a ticket and `lit restore` brings it
back; a purge cannot be undone.

What remains is a purge record that names the id and nothing else. Sync
carries the record to every store lit syncs with, and each store that receives
it drops the ticket, even one that edited the ticket in the meantime.
Restoring an older backup does not bring it back, and the id is never issued
again. Other tickets are not edited: a description, comment, follow-up or
duplicate that mentions the id keeps the mention.

An epic that still holds tickets is refused. Move them to another epic with
`lit parent set`, or purge them first.

Copies made before the purge are not reached: past Dolt commits, the store's
history on the git remote (refs/dolt/data), `lit snapshots`, and JSON backups
still hold the ticket until they are pruned.

At a terminal the purge asks for confirmation; elsewhere, pass --yes.
